MediFlow Symptom to Specialty Guide

Use this guide to find the right doctor for your symptoms.
If you are unsure which specialty to see, describe your symptom to the
assistant and it will recommend the right doctor for you.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CHEST, HEART, AND BLOOD PRESSURE SYMPTOMS
────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms: chest pain, chest tightness, heart palpitations, irregular
heartbeat, shortness of breath during activity, high blood pressure,
high cholesterol, dizziness, leg swelling, history of heart attack.

Recommended specialty: Cardiology

Best first choice: Dr. Tariq Butt — shorter wait time, available Monday
to Saturday 9am to 4pm, consultation fee PKR 2500. Good for preventive
cardiology, blood pressure, and cholesterol management.

For complex or urgent cardiac cases: Dr. Nadia Hussain — Pakistan's most
experienced cardiologist at MediFlow with 18 years experience and a
Fellowship from London Royal College. Specializes in interventional
cardiology, heart failure, and arrhythmia. Very high demand — book at
least 3 days in advance. Fee PKR 3000.

Emergency warning: if you have sudden severe chest pain, pain radiating
to the left arm or jaw, cold sweats, or difficulty breathing — do NOT
book an appointment. Call 1122 immediately or go to the nearest hospital
emergency room. This is a medical emergency.

────────────────────────────────────────────
GENERAL ILLNESS AND EVERYDAY HEALTH
────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms: fever, cough, cold, sore throat, body aches, fatigue,
headache, diabetes management, routine prescriptions, blood pressure
monitoring, general health checkup, weight management, general weakness,
stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, urinary symptoms.

Recommended specialty: General Medicine

Dr. Ahmed Raza — 15 years experience, specializes in diabetes and
hypertension. Available Monday to Saturday 9am to 6pm. Fee PKR 1000.
Recommended for chronic disease management and routine checkups.

Dr. Sara Malik — specializes in women's health, nutrition, and
preventive care. Available Monday to Saturday 10am to 7pm. Fee PKR 1000.
Recommended for female patients and nutrition consultations.

Dr. Kamran Iqbal — specializes in elderly care and chronic disease
follow-ups. Speaks Punjabi in addition to Urdu and English. Available
Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. Fee PKR 1000. Recommended for older
patients and patients with multiple ongoing conditions.

Walk-in friendly: General Medicine has the highest walk-in availability.
Expect 20 to 40 minute wait for walk-ins during off-peak hours.

────────────────────────────────────────────
CHILDREN AND INFANT HEALTH
────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms in children: fever, cold, cough, ear infection, stomach pain,
vomiting, diarrhea, skin rash, growth concerns, vaccination schedule,
asthma, nutritional problems, behavioral concerns, school health.

Recommended specialty: Pediatrics

Children under 12 are strongly recommended to see a pediatrician, not
a general medicine doctor.

Dr. Ayesha Khan — 14 years experience, specializes in newborn care,
childhood infections, and vaccinations. Very high demand. Available
Monday to Saturday 9am to 6pm. Fee PKR 1500.
Important: Dr. Ayesha Khan is very popular. Walk-in wait is often 60
minutes or more. Booking an appointment in advance is strongly
recommended for her.

Dr. Bilal Chaudhry — specializes in childhood asthma, nutritional
disorders, and school health issues. Speaks Pashto in addition to Urdu
and English. Available Tuesday to Sunday 10am to 7pm. Fee PKR 1200.
Good alternative to Dr. Ayesha Khan with shorter waiting times.

────────────────────────────────────────────
BONE, JOINT, AND MUSCLE CONDITIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms: joint pain, knee pain, back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain,

difficulty walking, post-surgery recovery, physiotherapy referral needed,
bone density concerns.

Recommended specialty: Orthopedics

Dr. Hina Javed — 16 years experience, specializes in joint replacement,
sports injuries, spine disorders, and fractures. Available Monday to
Saturday 10am to 6pm. Fee PKR 2500.

Dr. Faisal Sheikh — specializes in physiotherapy referrals, bone density
assessment, and fracture management. Available Monday to Saturday 9am to
5pm. Fee PKR 2000. Good choice if you need physiotherapy coordination.

────────────────────────────────────────────
WHEN YOU ARE UNSURE WHICH DOCTOR TO SEE
────────────────────────────────────────────

If you are not sure which specialty matches your symptoms, book General
Medicine first. The General Medicine doctor will examine you and provide
a referral to the appropriate specialist if needed. A referral letter
from General Medicine is recommended before seeing a specialist.

────────────────────────────────────────────
BEST TIMES TO VISIT (LESS WAITING)
────────────────────────────────────────────

Peak hours with longer waits: 10am to 12pm and 3pm to 5pm daily.
Off-peak hours with shorter waits: 9am to 10am and 5pm to 7pm.
Least busy day: Saturday afternoon and Sunday (limited services Sunday).
Busiest day: Monday and Wednesday mornings.

Booking an appointment in advance reduces wait time significantly
compared to walk-in. The AI assistant can check predicted wait times
for each slot before you confirm a booking.
