Doctor Availability and Wait Time Guide

This section helps patients understand expected waiting times and
the best way to book each doctor.

Dr. Ahmed Raza — General Medicine
Best for: Diabetes, hypertension, fever, general checkup
Walk-in availability: Good. Expect 20 to 30 minutes wait off-peak.
Advance booking recommended: Yes for specific time preference.
Typical booked appointment wait: 5 to 15 minutes.

Dr. Sara Malik — General Medicine
Best for: Female patients, nutrition, preventive care
Walk-in availability: Moderate. Expect 25 to 40 minutes off-peak.
Note: Preferred by female patients — slightly higher demand.
Typical booked appointment wait: 5 to 15 minutes.

Dr. Kamran Iqbal — General Medicine
Best for: Elderly patients, chronic disease follow-ups, Punjabi speakers
Walk-in availability: Good. Typically less busy than Dr. Raza and Dr. Malik.
Advance booking recommended: Especially for elderly patients.
Typical booked appointment wait: 5 to 10 minutes.

Dr. Nadia Hussain — Cardiology
Best for: Complex cardiac cases, heart failure, arrhythmia
Walk-in availability: Very limited. She is the most in-demand doctor
at MediFlow. Walk-ins are often turned away.
Advance booking required: Book at least 3 days in advance.
Typical booked appointment wait: 15 to 25 minutes due to complex cases.
Alternative: Dr. Tariq Butt for less complex cardiac cases.

Dr. Tariq Butt — Cardiology
Best for: Preventive cardiology, blood pressure, cholesterol
Walk-in availability: Moderate. Better walk-in option than Dr. Hussain.
Advance booking recommended: Book 1 to 2 days ahead for preferred time.
Typical booked appointment wait: 10 to 20 minutes.

Dr. Ayesha Khan — Pediatrics
Best for: Newborns, infants, childhood infections, vaccinations
Walk-in availability: Very limited. Walk-in wait often 60 minutes or more.
She is the most in-demand pediatrician at MediFlow.
Advance booking strongly recommended.
Alternative: Dr. Bilal Chaudhry has shorter waiting times.
Typical booked appointment wait: 15 to 30 minutes.

Dr. Bilal Chaudhry — Pediatrics
Best for: Childhood asthma, nutrition, school health, Pashto speakers
Walk-in availability: Good. Better walk-in option than Dr. Ayesha Khan.
Available Tuesday to Sunday — note he is NOT available on Mondays.
Typical booked appointment wait: 5 to 15 minutes.

Dr. Zara Siddiqui — Dermatology
Best for: Acne, eczema, psoriasis, hair loss, cosmetic procedures
Walk-in availability: Moderate. Starts at 11am.
Typical booked appointment wait: 10 to 15 minutes.

Dr. Usman Qureshi — Dermatology
Best for: Fungal infections, general skin, wound care
Walk-in availability: Good. Less demand than Dr. Siddiqui.
Available Monday to Friday only — not available weekends.
Typical booked appointment wait: 5 to 10 minutes.

Dr. Hina Javed — Orthopedics
Best for: Joint replacement, spine, sports injuries, fractures
Walk-in availability: Moderate.
Advance booking recommended for complex cases with imaging.
Typical booked appointment wait: 10 to 20 minutes.

Dr. Faisal Sheikh — Orthopedics
Best for: Physiotherapy coordination, bone density, fracture management
Walk-in availability: Good. Often less busy than Dr. Hina Javed.
Good choice if you need a physiotherapy referral.
Typical booked appointment wait: 10 to 15 minutes.

Referral Recommendations:
If you are unsure which specialist to see, book General Medicine first.
The General Medicine doctor will examine you and provide a formal
referral letter to the appropriate specialist. This is especially
recommended for first-time specialist visits.
