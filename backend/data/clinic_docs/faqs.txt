Frequently Asked Questions
 
Q: How long does a typical appointment take?
A: The consultation itself is 10 to 20 minutes. Total visit time including waiting is
   typically 30 to 60 minutes during peak hours which are 10am to 12pm and 3pm to 5pm.
   Off-peak visits in early morning or late afternoon are usually faster.
 
Q: Can I get a same-day appointment?
A: Yes, for urgent cases. Book via chat or call before 10am for best same-day
   availability. Popular doctors like Dr. Ayesha Khan for pediatrics and Dr. Nadia
   Hussain for cardiology often have full schedules. Alternatives will be suggested.
 
Q: What if I need to see multiple doctors in one visit?
A: Inform reception when booking. We can schedule both appointments on the same day.
   A referral letter from your General Physician is recommended for specialist visits.
 
Q: Are prescriptions and reports available digitally?
A: Prescriptions are provided on paper at the time of visit. Digital copies of medical
   records are available upon request with 48 hours notice.
 
Q: Does the clinic have a pharmacy?
A: Yes. A fully stocked pharmacy is on the ground floor and is open during clinic hours.
   Most prescribed medications are available. Pharmacist consultation is free.
 
Q: Can I get a medical certificate?
A: Yes. Medical certificates for work or school absence are issued after consultation.
   The fee is PKR 200.
 
Q: Do you offer home visit services?
A: Home visits are not currently available. For immobile patients we recommend
   booking an urgent appointment and arranging transport assistance.
 
Q: What happens for emergency walk-ins?
A: Go directly to reception and state the emergency. Triage is done immediately.
   Critical patients are seen ahead of scheduled appointments.
 
Q: Can I see a doctor without an appointment?
A: Walk-ins are accepted based on slot availability. Expected wait is 30 to 90 minutes.
   Booking an appointment in advance is strongly recommended.
 
Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: Cancel at least 2 hours before appointment for no charge. Late cancellations
   incur a PKR 200 fee. No-shows without notice may be charged the consultation fee.

Q: My appointment is in 1 hour, can I still cancel?
A: Yes, but a PKR 200 late cancellation fee will apply since it is within
   2 hours of your appointment time. You can cancel through the chat
   assistant or by calling 0800-MEDIFLOW. Rescheduling is always free.

Q: My appointment is in 3 hours, can I cancel for free?
A: Yes. Cancellations made more than 2 hours before the appointment time
   are completely free. Cancel through the chat or call 0800-MEDIFLOW.

Q: I missed my appointment, what happens?
A: Missing an appointment without any notice may result in the full
   consultation fee being charged on your next visit. If you need to
   cancel, please do so at least 2 hours before to avoid any charges.
   You can rebook a new appointment through the chat.

Q: Which doctor is best for diabetes?
A: Dr. Ahmed Raza and Dr. Kamran Iqbal in General Medicine both
   specialize in diabetes management. Dr. Ahmed Raza is available
   Monday to Saturday and Dr. Kamran Iqbal is available Monday to Friday.
   Both charge PKR 1000. Book through the assistant to check today's
   available slots.

Q: Which doctor should I see for high blood pressure?
A: High blood pressure is handled by both General Medicine and
   Cardiology. For a new diagnosis, start with General Medicine —
   Dr. Ahmed Raza specializes in hypertension. For complex or
   long-standing cases, Dr. Tariq Butt in Cardiology is recommended.

Q: I have chest pain right now. Should I book an appointment?
A: If you have sudden severe chest pain, pain spreading to your arm
   or jaw, difficulty breathing, or cold sweats — this is a medical
   emergency. Do NOT wait for an appointment. Call 1122 immediately
   or go to the nearest hospital emergency room.
   For mild, non-emergency chest discomfort, book Cardiology through
   the assistant.

Q: Can a child see a general medicine doctor instead of a pediatrician?
A: Children under 12 are strongly recommended to see a pediatrician.
   Our pediatric specialists Dr. Ayesha Khan and Dr. Bilal Chaudhry
   are trained specifically for children's health. If Dr. Ayesha Khan
   is fully booked, Dr. Bilal Chaudhry is a great alternative with
   shorter waiting times.

Q: How early should I arrive for my appointment?
A: Arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled time to complete
   registration at reception. Bring your CNIC or B-Form for children,
   insurance card if applicable, and any previous reports or
   prescriptions.

Q: What are the least busy times to visit?
A: The least busy times are early morning 9am to 10am and late
   afternoon 5pm to 7pm. Peak hours with the longest waits are
   10am to 12pm and 3pm to 5pm. Saturday afternoon is also
   relatively quiet. Booking an appointment in advance always
   reduces your wait compared to walking in.

Q: Can I see more than one doctor in the same visit?
A: Yes. Inform the assistant or reception when booking. We can
   schedule both appointments on the same day. A referral letter
   from your General Medicine doctor is recommended if you are
   seeing a specialist for the first time.

Q: I need a sick leave certificate for my employer. Can I get one?
A: Yes. A medical certificate for work absence is issued after
   your consultation. The fee is PKR 200. Let the doctor know
   you need a certificate during your appointment.

Q: Can I get a prescription refill without coming in?
A: Repeat prescriptions for chronic conditions require a follow-up
   visit every 3 months. You cannot get a new prescription without
   seeing the doctor. Walk-in or book a quick follow-up appointment
   through the assistant.

Q: I lost my prescription. Can I get a copy?
A: Yes. Lost prescriptions can be reissued at reception for a
   PKR 100 administrative fee. Bring your CNIC to verify identity.

Q: Does the clinic have a lab for blood tests?
A: Yes. MediFlow has an in-house diagnostic lab for blood tests,
   urine tests, and basic imaging. Lab tests are charged separately
   based on the test ordered by your doctor. Fasting is required
   for most blood tests — no food for 8 to 12 hours before the
   test, but water is allowed.

Q: Do you have a pharmacy inside the clinic?
A: Yes. A fully stocked pharmacy is on the ground floor and is
   open during all clinic hours. Most medications prescribed by
   MediFlow doctors are available. Pharmacist consultation is
   free of charge.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Yes. Free parking is available in the basement for the first
   2 hours. After that, the charge is PKR 50 per hour.
   Disabled parking bays are available at the ground level entrance.

Q: Can elderly patients get assistance?
A: Yes. Priority seating is reserved in the waiting area for
   elderly and pregnant patients. Wheelchair ramps are at the
   main entrance, the elevator provides access to all floors,
   and accessible washrooms are on every floor. Inform reception
   if you require mobility assistance. One attendant is permitted
   in the consultation room for elderly patients.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept cash (PKR), bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa,
   Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards.
   For insurance, we accept Jubilee Health, EFU Life, Adamjee,
   State Life, Sehat Sahulat Program, Zong Sehat Card, Jazz Health,
   and Allianz EFU.

Q: How much does a general medicine appointment cost?
A: General Medicine consultations are PKR 1000 to 1500 depending
   on the doctor. Cardiology is PKR 2500 to 3000. Pediatrics is
   PKR 1200 to 1500. Dermatology is PKR 1200 to 1500.
   Orthopedics is PKR 2000 to 2500. Lab tests are charged
   separately based on what is ordered.

Q: Mujhe kaunse doctor se milna chahiye? (Which doctor should I see?)
A: Aap apne symptoms batayein aur assistant sahi specialty suggest
   karega. Agar aap sure nahi hain to General Medicine se shuru
   karein — doctor examine karke zaroorat pare to specialist ko
   refer kar denge.

Q: Appointment kitni jaldi mil sakti hai? (How soon can I get an appointment?)
A: Usi din ki appointment urgent cases ke liye available ho sakti
   hai agar slots khali hoon. Subah 10 baje se pehle book karna
   best hai same-day availability ke liye. Assistant abhi available
   slots check kar sakta hai.
