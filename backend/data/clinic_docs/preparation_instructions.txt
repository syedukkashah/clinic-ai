Pre-Appointment Preparation Instructions
 
CARDIOLOGY CONSULTATION:
- Fast for 4 hours before appointment. Water is allowed.
- Bring previous ECG reports if available.
- Bring a complete list of current medications with dosages.
- Bring your health insurance card.
- Avoid caffeine on appointment day.
- Wear comfortable loose clothing for chest examination.
- If you have a pacemaker, inform reception when booking.
 
BLOOD TESTS (if ordered by doctor):
- Fast for 8 to 12 hours before blood draw.
- Water is allowed and encouraged.
- Bring previous test reports for comparison.
- Inform lab staff of any medications you are taking.
 
ORTHOPEDIC CONSULTATION:
- Bring any recent X-rays or MRI scans on CD or printed film.
- Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to affected area.
- Bring physiotherapy reports if you have been receiving therapy.
- Note when the pain started and what makes it better or worse.

PEDIATRIC VISIT:
- Bring the child's vaccination record (Red Book).
- Note temperature readings, symptoms, and how long they have lasted.
- Bring any previous prescriptions given to the child.
- Bring a favorite toy or snack to keep the child comfortable.
- Both parents may accompany for the consultation.
 
DERMATOLOGY APPOINTMENT:
- Do not apply heavy creams or ointments to affected skin on appointment day.
- Remove nail polish if nails are being examined.
- Bring photos of the skin condition taken at different stages if available.
- List any products you have used on the affected area.
 
GENERAL MEDICINE:
- Bring a list of all current medications.
- Note when symptoms started and how they have changed.
- Bring previous prescriptions and test reports.
- Fasting is not required unless the doctor has specified otherwise.
 
FOR ALL APPOINTMENTS:
- Arrive 10 minutes early to complete registration.
- Bring original CNIC or B-Form for children.
- Bring insurance card if applicable.
- Bring a trusted companion for elderly patients or surgical consultations.

GENERAL TIPS FOR ALL PATIENTS:

Before any appointment:
1. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete registration at reception.
2. Bring your original CNIC. Bring the child's B-Form for patients
   under 18.
3. Bring your insurance card if applicable.
4. Bring all previous test reports, prescriptions, and scan results
   in a folder — even from other hospitals.
5. Write down your symptoms and when they started before coming.
   Doctors appreciate patients who can describe their history clearly.
6. Bring a list of all medications you are currently taking,
   including the dose and how often you take them.
7. Bring a trusted family member if you are elderly, anxious,
   or expecting complex news.

WHAT TO EXPECT AT YOUR APPOINTMENT:
- You will be called by name from the waiting area when your turn arrives.
- The doctor's door will be closed during the consultation for privacy.
- Consultations typically last 10 to 20 minutes.
- The doctor will examine you, ask questions, and explain your diagnosis.
- Prescriptions and any test referrals will be provided at the end.
- You may ask questions — doctors at MediFlow encourage patient questions.
- After the consultation, collect any prescriptions and visit the
  pharmacy on the ground floor if needed.

IF YOU ARE RUNNING LATE:
- Call 0800-MEDIFLOW to inform the clinic.
- If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your slot may be given
  to a waiting patient. You may still be seen depending on availability.

IF YOU NEED AN INTERPRETER:
- All doctors at MediFlow speak Urdu and English fluently.
- Dr. Kamran Iqbal also speaks Punjabi.
- Dr. Bilal Chaudhry also speaks Pashto.
- Inform reception when booking if you need language assistance.

BRINGING CHILDREN TO THE CLINIC:
- Both parents may accompany a child in the consultation room.
- Bring the child's vaccination record (Red Book).
- Pack a favorite toy or snack to keep the child calm during waiting.
- Tell the doctor about any known allergies or reactions to medications.

FOLLOW-UP APPOINTMENTS:
- If your doctor refers you for tests, visit the in-house lab on the
  ground floor. Results are usually available within 24 hours.
- Repeat prescriptions for chronic conditions require a follow-up
  visit every 3 months. You cannot get repeat prescriptions remotely.
- For specialist referrals, book the specialist through the chat
  assistant after getting your referral letter.
